   - Integrate 5,246 model extractions with the 100 Gold Standard bills (with provenance flag).

     * dataset/lawmaking_events.csv   (tidy relational table, 1 row per event)
     * dataset/lawmaking_events.jsonl (nested structured JSON, 1 row per bill)
     * dataset/ddl_registry.csv       (synchronized bill metadata)
